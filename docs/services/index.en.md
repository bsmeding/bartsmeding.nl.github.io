---

## UniFi only

We **design, deploy, and support UniFi networks only** (Ubiquiti UniFi gateways, switches, and access points). That keeps monitoring, backups, firmware management, and remote support consistent across every engagement.

Other vendors or consumer mesh kits are outside our managed service scope.

---

## Hardware — optional

Hardware supply is **fully optional**:

- **We can supply UniFi hardware** as part of a project quote (design, procurement, and installation).
- **We can work with your existing UniFi gear** — we onboard what you already have, document the current setup, and deliver an **improvement report** with concrete recommendations (coverage, segmentation, lifecycle, and upgrade paths).

Managed monthly packages assume UniFi equipment we can reach and manage remotely. Project pricing covers design, hardware (if supplied), and on-site work separately.
