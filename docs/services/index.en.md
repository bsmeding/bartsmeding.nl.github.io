## UniFi preferred, enterprise by agreement

We **prefer UniFi** (Ubiquiti gateways, switches, and access points) for managed residential and hospitality networks. UniFi fits our **automation** stack: monitoring, backups, lifecycle management, and remote support scale efficiently — which is how we keep **monthly costs manageable**. That level of operations is **not achievable** with standard consumer mesh or ISP routers.

For specific requirements we can also deploy **enterprise network equipment** **by agreement** — vendors such as **Arista**, **Juniper**, **Cisco**, and comparable platforms. Scope, tooling integration, and pricing are agreed upfront; enterprise environments are always **custom**, not the standard monthly packages listed on this site.

Consumer mesh kits and unsupported home-grade gear are outside our managed service scope.

The **monthly packages** on this site are scoped for **UniFi** environments we can manage remotely.

---

## Hardware — optional

Hardware supply is **fully optional**:

- **We can supply UniFi hardware** as part of a project quote (design, procurement, and installation).
- **We can work with your existing UniFi gear** — we onboard what you already have, document the current setup, and deliver an **improvement report** with concrete recommendations (coverage, segmentation, lifecycle, and upgrade paths).

Enterprise hardware (Arista, Juniper, Cisco, …) is quoted per project. Project pricing covers design, hardware (if supplied), and on-site work separately from monthly fees.
